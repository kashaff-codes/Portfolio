Open Source · Supabase
supabase/supavisor #1051
Chasing a silent data-loss bug into the pooler

SERIALIZABLE transactions through Supabase's transaction pooler report success and silently discard their writes. I reproduced it on a fresh project; a Supabase engineer routed the issue to the pooler repository within hours.

  • Disproved my own hypothesis — not SSI timing, but client-side prepared statements
  • 10/16 vs 0/16 — failures with prepare on versus off; the surplus “successful” commits exactly accounted for the lost rows
  • Shipped a workaround — plus a psycopg2 control and the probe that failed, because its null result bounds the timing window
